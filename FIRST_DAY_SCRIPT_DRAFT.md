# Hell Story - First In-Game Day Script Draft

This draft consolidates the notes from `hell story clusterfuck.pdf` into one playable first-day structure.

Goal for Day 1:
- Get the player from boring mortal life into Hell.
- Introduce all five demonesses.
- Establish the main mystery: the PC should be suffering like a normal soul, but is not.
- Establish the bureaucratic pressure: Hell needs to stabilize the PC's entropy before the CBA notices.
- Give the player the H-Phone.
- Let the player choose one Day 1 date: Lily, Lucy, Sam, Belle, or Molly.
- End with an Unknown Number / Big D plot hook.

Tone:
- Dark comedy dating sim.
- Profane, absurd, horny-adjacent, but keep the first day more funny/flirty than explicit.
- The demons are dangerous and ridiculous, but each should show one small crack of humanity.

## Day 1 Structure

1. Mortal Office Intro
2. Commute to Damnation
3. Crash Landing in Hell
4. Demoness Introductions
5. Infernal Orientation / Stat Setup
6. HELL-iday Inn and H-Phone
7. Main Plot Seed: Unknown Number
8. Daytime Free Activity
9. Night H-Phone Date Choice
10. One Date Route
11. End-of-Day Main Plot Hook

## Scene 1: Mortal Office Intro

Location: Aeroplex Supply Solutions, cubicle farm.

Purpose:
- Show the PC's mortal life is soul-killing before literal damnation.
- Let the PC name themselves.
- Set up the joke that Hell is somehow less depressing than office work.

Script:

Narration:
The fluorescent lights buzz like trapped insects above a maze of beige cubicles.

Narration:
Your monitor displays a spreadsheet so dense it seems less like data and more like a curse.

You:
Thanks for holding, this is {{player.name}} with Aeroplex Supply Solutions. One moment while I pull up your account details.

Customer:
I need those EKG leads by tomorrow and you people never have anything in stock!

You:
Absolutely, I understand how frustrating that must be.

Narration:
You do not understand. You have not understood anything for roughly three fiscal quarters.

You:
Six more hours.

Narration:
Six hours later, you remove your headset with the haunted delicacy of a bomb technician.

You:
Freedom. Temporary, legally distinct freedom.

Narration:
Outside, the city sidewalk is wet, gray, and packed with commuters who all look like they lost a fight with a printer.

Street Preacher:
Sinners! Blasphemers! Hell awaits! Eternal punishment! Unquenchable fire!

You:
Buddy, I just got out of a nine-hour headset shift. You are going to have to sell me on the downside.

Optional class/stat prompt:
Belle's later orientation can ask the player what kind of mortal they were:
- Metalhead: STR/CON flavor
- Gamer: DEX/INT flavor
- Artist: WIS/RIZZ flavor
- Sales/Lawyer: RIZZ/INT flavor

## Scene 2: Commute to Damnation

Location: Highway, car, rain-slick road.

Purpose:
- Comedically overdo the PC surviving impossible dangers.
- Make the final death stupid: banana peel.
- Hint that something is already bending reality around the PC.

Script:

Narration:

You:

You:

You:
Okay. Cool. Normal Tuesday.

Narration:
The highway collapses into a sinkhole.

You:
That is less normal.

Narration:

You:
What the actual fu-

You:
I'M SORRY! I DIDN'T MEAN TO CUT YOU OFF OR WHATEVER!

Narration:
Explosions. Drifts. Screaming. A brief moment where reality pixelates around you like the universe is buffering.

No no no. I am not dying to lag.

Narration:
Somehow, you make it home.

You:
I made it. I actually made it through that entire impossible gauntlet of bullsh-

Narration:
Your foot hits a banana peel on the welcome mat.

You:
...No.

Narration:
Concrete rushes up to greet you with professional enthusiasm.

Cut to black.

# Date Route 1: Lily - Hell Chili's

Theme:
- Lily is lusty/flirty, but the subtext is that she treats romance as transactional because she thinks love always is.
- First date should be funny, tempting, and a little emotionally hollow at the end.

Location:
Hell Chili's, but the neon sign is broken so it reads "Hehi's."

Script:

You:
So this is where you wanted to meet?

Lily:

You look up at the sign. It was clearly supposed to say "Hell Chili's," but half the letters are burned out. It now reads: HEHI'S.

You:
Your favorite place, huh?

Lily:

You:

Lily:
Exactly. In Hell, happy hour is a legally contested state of mind.

Narration:
Inside, the walls are decorated with cursed license plates, novelty pitchforks, and framed photos of people regretting appetizers.

Lily:
So, first date questions. Mortal edition. Favorite color, deepest fear, most embarrassing desire, blood type.

You ask blood type on first dates?

Lily:
Only when I am being classy.

Choice 1:
- Ask Lily a sincere question.
- Flirt back aggressively.

If sincere:

You:
What do you actually like about this place?

Lily:
Actually?

Narration:
For half a second, her smile slips.

Lily:
Nobody expects anything real here. It is all plastic menus and bad decisions. There is comfort in that.

You:
That sounded almost honest.

Lily:
Careful. Keep listening like that and I might develop standards.

If flirt:

You:
I thought you brought me here because the lighting makes me look edible.

Lily:
It does. Tragically edible.

Narration:
She leans close enough that the candle flame between you bends toward her.

Lily:
But do not get cocky. I have sent cuter mortals to worse restaurants.

Shared continuation:

Hellbilly Server:

You:
What is screaming about the fajitas?

Hellbilly Server:
The fajitas.

Lily:
Two drinks. Something pink, dangerous, and financially irresponsible.

Choice 2:
- Suggest actually eating dinner.
- Suggest leaving before the fajitas testify.

Lily:
Normal?

Narration:

Ending:

That good?

Lily:
Maybe next time we try somewhere with fewer laminated menus.

Affection result:
