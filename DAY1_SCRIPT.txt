HELL STORY — DAY 1 (PLAYABLE SCRIPT)
=====================================
Readable copy of the Day 1 rewrite in js/script.js (from "PLAYABLE DAY 1 REWRITE" to the end of the file).
The game code is the source of truth, not this file. The older prototype block in the code is not included here.
Player lines are marked YOU. {{player.name}} is the name the player types at the start.

SCENE: OFFICE INTRO
[The fluorescent lights buzz like trapped insects above a maze of beige cubicles.]
[Your monitor displays a spreadsheet so dense it seems less like data and more like a curse.]
[Name input: "What is your name?" — the answer is saved as {{player.name}}.]
YOU: Thanks for holding, this is {{player.name}} with Aeroplex Supply Solutions. One moment while I pull up your account details.
CALLER: This is unacceptable! I need those EKG leads by tomorrow and you people never have anything in stock!
YOU: Absolutely, I understand how frustrating that must be.
CALLER: You do not understand. You have not understood anything for roughly three fiscal quarters.
YOU: Six more hours.
[Six hours later, you remove your headset with the haunted delicacy of a bomb technician.]
YOU: Freedom. Temporary, legally distinct freedom.
[Outside, a wild-eyed street preacher shoves a hand-painted sign in your face.]
STREET PREACHER: Sinners! Blasphemers! Hell awaits! Eternal punishment!
YOU: Buddy, I just got out of a nine-hour headset shift. You are going to have to sell me on the downside.

SCENE: COMMUTE
[Your beat-up sedan tears down the interstate, music blaring, windshield wipers losing a war against the rain.]
YOU: Okay. Home. Shower. Food. Maybe stare at a wall for two hours and call it self-care.
[Ahead, a semi-truck jackknifes across four lanes.]
YOU: Whoa, shit!
[You swerve at the last second. The car lifts onto two wheels, threads through flying sparks, and lands clean.]
YOU: Okay. Cool. Normal Tuesday.
[The highway collapses into a sinkhole.]
YOU: That is less normal.
[You floor it. For one impossible second, a trail of hellfire burns under your tires and carries you across.]
YOU: What the actual fu-
[A biker gang bursts out of the rain behind you, guns blazing.]
YOU: I AM SORRY! I DID NOT MEAN TO CUT YOU OFF OR WHATEVER!
[Explosions. Drifts. Screaming. A brief moment where reality pixelates like the universe is buffering.]
YOU: No no no. I am not dying to lag.
[Somehow, you make it home.]
YOU: I made it. I actually made it through that entire impossible gauntlet of bullsh-
[Your foot hits a banana peel on the welcome mat.]
YOU: ...No.
[Concrete rushes up to greet you with professional enthusiasm.]
[HELL STORY logo sting.]

SCENE: CRASH LANDING
[You fall through smoke, heat, screaming wind, and at least three layers of municipal zoning violation.]
[You crash through a wooden announcer table in an explosion of splinters and demonic confetti.]
[All five girls appear at once: Molly, Sam, Lily, Belle, Lucy.]
MOLLY: OH YEAH! Did everybody see that supreme spitfire bomb through my custom announcer table?!
SAM: For the last time, Molly, this is not one of your ridiculous wrestling exhibitions. That was an actual soul.
LILY: And still intact. Impressive. Also potentially snack material.
BELLE: A human soul crash-landed directly into the Ninth Circle? That defies every trans-dimensional intake ordinance I have ever catalogued.
LUCY: Or it means our onboarding department finally processed my VIP requisition.
SAM: They do not look VIP. They look like raw recruitment material.
MOLLY: They look like they owe me one announcer table.
YOU: Am I dead?
LUCY: Technically, yes.
YOU: Am I in Hell?
LILY: Technically, deliciously yes.
YOU: Am I supposed to be on fire?
BELLE: That is actually an excellent question, and I hate that I do not have the answer.
[For the first time, all five demons look worried.]

SCENE: INTRODUCTIONS
[Lucy steps forward.]
LUCY: Lucy. Executive authority, strategic oversight, infernal operations. If something in Hell is broken, it is usually because someone failed to obey my very clear memo.
YOU: So you are in charge?
LUCY: Obviously.
[Sam steps forward.]
SAM: Sam. Field Marshal of the Fallen Host. You will address me with respect, posture, and a basic willingness to survive.
YOU: Do I get a helmet?
SAM: Not with that tone.
[Lily steps forward.]
LILY: Lily. Vice, temptation, pleasure, bad decisions, good stories. I make eternity bearable, or at least memorable.
YOU: That sounds like a warning.
LILY: That was me being polite.
[Belle steps forward.]
BELLE: Belle. Archivist, compliance researcher, custodian of arcane records. I am sorry about all of this, unless you caused it, in which case I am still sorry but more judgmental.
[Molly steps forward.]
MOLLY: Molly! Demolition sweetheart, wrestling menace, certified chaos enthusiast. If it explodes, suplexes, or screams, I probably know a guy.
YOU: I have never been more afraid of the phrase sweetheart.
MOLLY: Aww, you are learning already!

SCENE: ORIENTATION
BELLE: Before anything else, we need to classify you.
YOU: As what?
BELLE: That is the problem.
LUCY: Every soul has a category. Gluttons, tyrants, frauds, influencers who filmed apology videos in parked cars.
LILY: People who say we should hang sometime and never mean it.
SAM: Men who leave shopping carts loose in parking lots.
BELLE: That is actually one of our largest archive wings.
YOU: So what am I?
BELLE: Unclear. The system lists you as: Pending, unstable, probably expensive.
LUCY: That is not a category. That is a budget meeting.
BELLE: I am going to run a manual aptitude spread. Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, and...
LILY: Rizz.
BELLE: I hate that the official form says Rizz.

SCENE: STAT ALLOCATION
[Stat allocation screen: distribute 10 points across STR / DEX / CON / INT / WIS / RIZZ. You can only confirm when 0 points remain.]
BELLE: Okay. That is...not good, but it is data.
YOU: Do I pass?
LUCY: Nobody passes Hell. They merely become actionable.

SCENE: H-PHONE
LUCY: We need to get our little anomaly settled before the Celestial Bureaucracy Administration gets a whiff of this.
YOU: The what?
SAM: The CBA. Heaven auditors. Merciless. Humorless. Armed with clipboards capable of severing immortal essence.
BELLE: If they discover an unprocessed mortal in the Ninth Circle, they could relocate all of us.
MOLLY: And not fun relocate. Clipboard relocate.
LILY: Which is tragic, because I have outfits that deserve better lighting than a compliance hearing.
LUCY: For now, you are a VIP guest of Hell LLC.
YOU: That sounds expensive.
BELLE: Technically, your soul is collateral.
YOU: That sounds expensive.
SAM: You will need this.
[Sam hands you a black glass phone. It is warm, heavy, and faintly screaming.]
SAM: H-Phone. Obsidian glass. Recycled legion armor casing. Inferno OS.
LILY: The screen is powered by tortured souls, but in a cute way.
BELLE: The in-app purchases also drain your soul.
YOU: Also in a cute way?
BELLE: No.
LUCY: Every night, you will receive invitations through Agenda-666. Choose one of us. We gather affinity data and stabilize your entropy.
YOU: You are making dating sound like a tax audit.
LUCY: Most intimacy is.

SCENE: UNKNOWN MESSAGE
[Drew, the three-headed hyena clerk, shoves you into your suite and growls something about complimentary suffering.]
[The room is luxurious in the way a haunted casino is luxurious. Velvet sheets. Bone chandelier. A minibar labeled DO NOT MAKE EYE CONTACT.]
YOU: Okay. Dead. Hell. Hot demons. Government problem. Phone made of screaming.
YOU: *BUZZ*
UNKNOWN: Ah, there you are.
YOU: Who is this?
UNKNOWN: Someone with a vested interest in your continued instability.
YOU: That is an insane way to introduce yourself.
UNKNOWN: You are not here by accident. You are also not here by ordinary damnation. The reason you are not burning will become clear.
UNKNOWN: For now, let Hell finest try to stabilize you. It will be funny.
YOU: Is this a threat?
UNKNOWN: Think of it as paternal encouragement from a stranger.
[The message deletes itself.]
YOU: I hate that paternal was the worst word in there.

SCENE: DAY LOOP — MORNING
[Morning arrives in Hell without sunlight. Instead, the sky turns from black-red to slightly more judgmental red.]
YOU: I slept maybe forty minutes and dreamed about my W-2 biting me.
H-PHONE: AGENDA-666 NOTICE: Entropy Stabilization Activities Available.
H-PHONE: You have 3 daytime blocks. Training, work, and hangouts spend time. Phone and shopping are free.
LUCY: Do at least one productive thing today. If the CBA asks, I want to say we made an effort.
SAM: Train. Weakness is just failure before it gets honest.
LILY: Or come relax. Stress is terrible for the skin, and yours still technically has mortal warranty.
BELLE: The Archives are open if you want answers. Or dust inhalation. Usually both.
MOLLY: HEY. WANNA DO SOMETHING STUPID?
[You have 3 daytime blocks. Each activity costs 1 block. Checking the H-Phone is free. Activities you have already done drop off the list. When your blocks run out, night falls.]
CHOICE: "Train with Sam (STR)" -> Day activity: Gym with Sam
CHOICE: "Obstacle course with Lucy (DEX)" -> Day activity: Parkour with Lucy
CHOICE: "Infernal endurance trial (CON)" -> Day activity: Pool trial
CHOICE: "Archives with Belle (INT)" -> Day activity: Library with Belle
CHOICE: "Meditation with Molly (WIS)" -> Day activity: Meditation with Molly
CHOICE: "Social club with Lily (RIZZ)" -> Day activity: Social club with Lily
CHOICE: "Earn souls at Hell temp work" -> Day activity: Temp work (+5 souls)
CHOICE: "Check H-Phone" -> H-Phone menu (free, no block spent)

SCENE: DAY ACTIVITY — GYM WITH SAM
SAM: Spot me, recruit. If the weights scream, scream louder.
[A barbell chained in silver rattles on the rack. Every plate has a tiny face and every tiny face looks judgmental.]
SAM: Lift clean. Breathe correctly. Do not flirt with spinal injury.
YOU: That was my whole plan.
[Stat check: STRENGTH vs 5.]
-- If the check passes --
[You get the bar up. The screaming weights go briefly silent, like they are embarrassed on your behalf.]
SAM: Adequate. Disturbingly adequate.
YOU: Was that a compliment?
SAM: It was a tactical observation. Treasure it quietly.
[Result: +1 STR, +1 Sam affection.]
-- If the check fails --
[The barbell rises three inches, makes direct eye contact, and returns to the rack with legal authority.]
SAM: You survived. That is the lowest possible form of victory.
YOU: I will put it on my resume.
SAM: Do not. I will check.
[Result: +1 STR.]
[Costs 1 daytime block.]

SCENE: DAY ACTIVITY — PARKOUR WITH LUCY
LUCY: The obstacle course is legally distinct from a murder maze.
[The course unfolds across rooftops, swinging chains, tax forms, and one rotating saw labeled TEAM BUILDING.]
LUCY: Fast is useful. Controlled is rare. Try to be both, or at least entertaining.
[Stat check: DEXTERITY vs 5.]
-- If the check passes --
[You clear the last gap, land badly, recover worse, and somehow make it look intentional.]
LUCY: Efficient. I may put that in a memo.
YOU: Please title it: Mortal Briefly Useful.
LUCY: Already drafted.
[Result: +1 DEX, +1 Lucy affection.]
-- If the check fails --
[You miss a platform and are caught by a safety net made of disappointed hands.]
LUCY: You fell with memorable commitment.
YOU: That sounds almost positive.
LUCY: It is not. But it is memorable.
[Result: +1 DEX.]
[Costs 1 daytime block.]

SCENE: DAY ACTIVITY — POOL TRIAL
[The Infernal Wellness Annex offers a free endurance assessment, which is suspicious because nothing in Hell is free except consequences.]
[An imp in a visor points you toward a treadmill made of bone, brass, and obvious OSHA violations.]
IMP ATTENDANT: Run until the machine respects you, collapses, or requests legal counsel.
YOU: Which one usually happens first?
IMP ATTENDANT: Depends how funny you are.
[Stat check: CONSTITUTION vs 5.]
-- If the check passes --
[You keep running after the treadmill catches fire, which seems to impress the treadmill more than the staff.]
IMP ATTENDANT: Congratulations. The machine has marked you as technically alive.
YOU: That is the nicest thing a machine has ever said to me.
[Result: +1 CON.]
-- If the check fails --
[You last almost a full minute before sliding off the treadmill with the grace of wet laundry.]
IMP ATTENDANT: Do not worry. The floor signs the liability waiver for you.
YOU: I hate premium wellness.
[Result: +1 CON.]
[Costs 1 daytime block.]

SCENE: DAY ACTIVITY — LIBRARY WITH BELLE
BELLE: Please do not breathe on the cursed indexes. They are sensitive.
[Belle places three books in front of you. One whispers your name, one bites the table, and one has already filed a complaint.]
BELLE: We are looking for a missing clause in your damnation record. If it screams, cite it properly.
[Stat check: INTELLIGENCE vs 5.]
-- If the check passes --
[You spot a recursive footnote hiding inside another footnote like a coward.]
BELLE: Oh. That was actually a very good observation.
YOU: I understood half of it.
BELLE: In Archives work, that is basically fluency.
[Result: +1 INT, +1 Belle affection.]
-- If the check fails --
[You open a book upside down. The book takes this personally and turns your bookmark into ash.]

[Result: +1 INT.]
[Costs 1 daytime block.]

SCENE: DAY ACTIVITY — MEDITATION WITH MOLLY
MOLLY: Meditation means sitting still until the urge to body-slam destiny passes.
[The garden is quiet except for distant screams, wind chimes made of tiny ribs, and Molly cracking her knuckles at enlightenment.]
MOLLY: Close your eyes. If you see your inner demon, suplex it with kindness.
[Stat check: WISDOM vs 5.]
-- If the check passes --
[For one impossible moment, Hell goes quiet. Even Molly whispers.]

MOLLY: Me neither, but I felt it in my punching soul.
[Result: +1 WIS, +1 Molly affection.]
-- If the check fails --
[Your mind clears completely, then immediately fills with the phrase bone chandelier on repeat.]

[Result: +1 WIS.]
[Costs 1 daytime block.]

SCENE: DAY ACTIVITY — SOCIAL CLUB WITH LILY

[The social club is packed with demons exchanging gossip, drinks, and eye contact with dangerous financial terms.]

[Stat check: RIZZ vs 5.]

[You work the room so well a duke of pestilence asks if you have representation.]

[Result: +1 RIZZ, +1 Lily affection.]
-- If the check fails --
[You deliver a compliment so awkward it somehow becomes a minor haunting.]

[Result: +1 RIZZ.]
[Costs 1 daytime block.]
